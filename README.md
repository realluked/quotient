# Quotient

Duolingo-style revision for **Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics (0580)**, made by **realluked**.

**Play it here: https://realluked.github.io/quotient/**

- 76 bite-size lessons covering every topic: number, algebra, graphs and calculus, shape and space, trigonometry and vectors, probability and statistics
- Core or Extended: switch any time; Extended content is clearly tagged
- Step-by-step worked examples, "watch out" warnings for common mistakes, and full working when you slip
- Answers checked properly: fractions, surds, standard form, algebraic expressions, coordinates and vectors
- Diagrams drawn for every topic: graphs, triangles, circles, solids, Venn and tree diagrams, histograms and more
- Exam practice: structured questions with mark schemes, plus a no-calculator paper and a calculator paper
- Check your written answers for free with Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, then paste the marking back in
- Recall deck with spaced repetition for formulas, facts, methods and key words, plus Mental Rush and Formula Rush
- Optional account: play as a guest, or log in to sync progress across devices (one account also works on Bitwise, Inkling and Wavelength)

All questions, explanations and diagrams are original. Not affiliated with Cambridge International.

Also by realluked: [Bitwise](https://realluked.github.io/bitwise/) for IGCSE Computer Science, [Inkling](https://realluked.github.io/inkling/) for IGCSE English and [Wavelength](https://realluked.github.io/wavelength/) for IGCSE Physics.
